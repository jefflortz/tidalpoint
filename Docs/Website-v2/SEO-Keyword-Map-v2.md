## Competitive search observations

The observed New England/Massachusetts results include:

- CenterPoint Business Advisors: exit planning, valuation, brokerage, and New England coverage.
- BayState Business Brokers: a dedicated construction/specialized-trades page naming HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, and other trades.
- Masswell Capital Advisors: dedicated commercial/industrial service-business exit content.
- Business Brokers Group: dedicated HVAC, plumbing, and electrical paths for Rhode Island/Massachusetts.
- FCBB Boston: preparation, valuation, and selling guides.

The result set confirms that broad “business broker” intent is competitive and often served by established brokerages, directories, listings, and regional firms. TPP’s differentiating opportunity is the combination of operator-led value creation and active buyer search—not a generic brokerage page.

## Alternative reliable research sources

1. Google Ads Keyword Planner with Massachusetts and regional targeting.
2. Google Trends for relative direction and seasonality, not absolute volume.
3. Google Search Console for real TPP impressions/clicks after new pages launch.
4. Bing Webmaster Tools keyword data if configured.
5. Manual SERP review for directories, local packs, result type, and competitor positioning.
6. Customer-call and CRM language for high-intent phrasing that low-volume tools may miss.

## Remaining gaps

- Exact local monthly volume and difficulty.
- SERP feature prevalence by target term.
- Backlink/domain authority gap versus regional competitors.
- Which verticals produce the best commercial fit.
- Whether users search “broker,” “M&A advisor,” “exit planning,” or “sell my business” at TPP’s deal size.

Do not lock final title tags, create vertical pages, or forecast traffic until the P0/P1 terms are validated in Keyword Planner or Semrush.
