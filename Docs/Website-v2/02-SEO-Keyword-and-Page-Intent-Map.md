## Prioritization rules

1. Commercial journey and user intent take priority over raw volume.
2. A single primary intent is assigned to each indexable page.
3. Geographic modifiers belong on substantive regional pages, not duplicated doorway pages.
4. “Valuation” content must distinguish indicative education from formal valuation services.
5. “Business broker” and “M&A advisor” terms require legal/service-model confirmation before optimization.
6. Exact volume and difficulty must be added from Semrush, Google Ads Keyword Planner, or another approved source before final copy and metadata are locked.
