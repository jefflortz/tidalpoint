# Website 2.0 Page-Level Content Specifications

## November 23, 2026 implementation-ready revision

This section supersedes the shorter page summaries below where it differs.

### Homepage

- Keep approved H1, descriptor, tagline and two primary CTAs.
- Opening copy must plainly state that TPP provides business brokerage and helps owners prepare for a future sale.
- Add a visible valuation entry module linking to `/resources/what-is-my-business-worth`.
- Preserve three distinct entry intents: Sell, Build, Valuation.
- Required internal links: all three journeys, Who We Help, About, regional hub, one approved insight and Contact.

### Sell Your Business

- **H1:** We Don't Just List Your Business. We Find the Right Buyers.
- **Opening:** “Looking for a business broker who does more than put your company on a listing website? Tidal Point Partners helps owners sell their businesses by actively searching for prospective buyers, preparing the business for sale and guiding the transaction from start to finish.” Final wording remains subject to legal/service-scope approval.
- Active buyer search must appear within the first two screens.
- Explain identify, approach and evaluate steps using process language rather than unverified network or financing claims.
- Explain operator-led positioning through earnings quality, owner dependence, customers, leadership, systems, risk and growth.
- Link to the valuation resource for owners who are still establishing expectations.
- CTA: **Let's Talk About Selling Your Business.**

### Build Business Value

- **H1:** Make Your Business Worth More Before You Sell.
- Name the structured offer **Value Acceleration Program** only after the name and scope are owner-approved.
- Promise three tangible deliverables: an understanding of value drivers and risks; a practical 12-month improvement roadmap; experienced support through implementation.
- Describe assessment, priorities, owners, milestones, cadence and evidence of progress.
- Avoid coaching language and any guaranteed increase in valuation or sale outcome.
- Link to valuation education and Sell when the owner's horizon changes.

### What Is My Business Worth?

- **URL:** `/resources/what-is-my-business-worth`
- **H1:** What Is My Business Worth?
- **SEO role:** substantive Release 1 educational resource for business valuation, business valuation services, and worth questions.
- Explain earnings/cash flow, owner dependence, customer concentration, leadership, systems, risk, growth, market conditions and deal structure.
- State why methods differ by business and context.
- Clearly distinguish a preliminary discussion from a certified valuation, appraisal or fairness opinion.
- Do not offer an automated calculator.
- Conversion path A: **Ready to sell — Request a confidential business value and sale consultation.**
- Conversion path B: **Planning ahead — Explore opportunities to improve business value.**

### Required FAQs

- Sell: What does a business broker do? How is confidentiality protected? How are prospective buyers evaluated? What information is needed? What if I am not ready yet?
- Build: What is included in the assessment? What happens during the 12 months? Who participates? How is progress measured? Does the program guarantee an increase in value?
- Valuation: How do buyers value privately held businesses? Which earnings measure is used? How does owner dependence affect value? Is this a formal appraisal? What should I do if I want to sell soon?

### Release 2 content

- One substantive `/industries/trades-and-service-businesses` page covering HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, construction and related services.
- One `/resources/how-to-prepare-and-sell-your-business` guide.
- Strengthen the current regional hub; do not create six thin state pages.

### Content and metadata gates

- `business broker`, brokerage authority, valuation services, buyer qualification and geographic coverage require approval recorded in the compliance checklist.
- All page claims must be either verified facts or bounded process descriptions.
- Existing H1 approvals remain unchanged solely for SEO.
- Metadata follows the revised SEO map.

## Global messaging rules

- Descriptor: **Business Value Creation & M&A Advisory**.
- Tagline: **Build Value. Realize Value.**
- Use plain language before M&A terminology.
- Describe trades and service businesses as primary examples, not the only eligible companies.
- State the baseline as approximately $2M annual revenue only after owner confirmation.
- “Operating Partner” is supporting differentiation, not the primary service category.
- Never guarantee valuation increases, sale timing, buyer availability, financing, or closing.
- Separate verified facts from aspirational positioning.

## Page specifications

### Homepage

- **Primary audience:** owner deciding whether to sell now or prepare for later.
- **Primary action:** choose Sell or Build.
- **Required copy:** approved H1 and supporting copy from the implementation brief.
- **Required proof:** operator experience, approved firm facts, regional presence.
- **SEO role:** brand/category hub, not a duplicate of either service page.
- **Internal links:** both journey pages, clients, About, one Build article, one Sell article when available, regional hub, Contact.

### Sell Your Business

- **Audience:** owner considering a near- or medium-term sale.
- **Primary question:** Can TPP find and help qualify the right buyers while representing the business well?
- **Required sections:** fit, operator-led positioning, sale preparation, active outreach, qualification, confidentiality, negotiation/diligence/closing support, FAQ, inquiry.
- **Required definitions:** what “active buyer search” and “financially qualified” mean operationally.
- **CTA:** Let’s Talk About Selling Your Business.
- **Compliance gate:** legal service model, licensing, fee language, network claims, transaction proof.

### Build Business Value

- **Audience:** owner who may sell later and wants a stronger, more transferable business.
- **Primary question:** What will TPP assess, improve, and measure over 12 months?
- **Required sections:** assessment, value drivers/risks, roadmap, supported implementation, measurement, readiness, FAQ, inquiry.
- **CTA:** Let’s Talk About Building Business Value.
- **Guardrail:** no generic coaching language and no guaranteed valuation outcome.

### Services `/services`

- **New label:** How We Help.
- **Role:** short comparison hub for Sell and Build, including when an owner may move from Build to Sell.
- **Do not:** repeat the full service pages or preserve the old generic Operating Partner offer verbatim.

### Clients `/clients`

- **New label:** Who We Help.
- **Content:** ownership, approximate scale, industries/examples, geography, trigger situations, fit/non-fit, journey routing.
- **Guardrail:** remove the unsupported fixed $5M–$50M profile unless a secondary segment is deliberately retained.

### About

- Explain the firm, legal entity/DBA, operator-led differentiation, values, team/network, and regional commitment.
- Connect operator experience to recognizing value drivers and representing a business credibly.
- Avoid implying transaction credentials that have not been verified.

### Jeff profile

- Preserve verified Navy, CEO, public-company, acquisition, operating, and education facts.
- Add relevance to value creation and owner decision-making.
- Do not convert operating experience into an unverified brokerage track record.

### Articles

- Add categories: Build Business Value, Exit Readiness, Sell Your Business, Leadership & Owner Independence, Growth & Operations, Regional Market.
- Every article gets a single primary journey CTA where relevant.
- Existing articles retain URLs and substantive content unless editorial review justifies changes.

### Locations

- Each page must contain unique local business context, service relevance, and proof.
- Link to both journeys and the regional hub.
- Avoid duplicated city-name copy and unsupported local market claims.

### Contact

- Start with Sell/Build selection or use two separate form entry points.
- State confidentiality and expected response behavior accurately.
- Show phone/email fallback immediately if the HubSpot embed is delayed.

## Metadata template guidance

- Titles: primary intent + differentiator/region where useful + brand.
- Descriptions: audience, outcome, and next step; no unsupported superlatives.
- One canonical per page.
- Open Graph copy aligned to visible page content.
- Structured data must describe actual services and approved claims only.

## Content dependencies

- Owner confirmation of baseline persona and geography.
- Legal confirmation of brokerage/M&A terminology.
- Verified buyer-search and qualification process.
- Approved proof, case material, testimonials, and credentials.
- Final fee/process details for FAQs.
