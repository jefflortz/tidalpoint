### Release 1 publication tests

- Sell metadata and first-screen copy use approved regulated terminology only.
- Valuation page explicitly says when the content/discussion is not a formal appraisal or certified valuation.
- No calculator, multiple or outcome estimate appears without a validated methodology and approved scope.
- No claim of buyer preapproval, financing, network size, transaction volume or guaranteed result.
- Geography matches documented authority on every page, schema object and footer.
- Forms and analytics collect only approved fields with accurate privacy/consent treatment.
- Proof placeholders used in prototypes are removed from production rather than published.

### SEO safeguards checklist

- Preserve all indexed article and location URLs.
- Preserve apex canonical, redirects, metadata utilities and structured-data implementation.
- Correct sitemap `lastmod` only during the authorized build and test with real content dates.
- Add no state or vertical page without unique substance and approved service coverage.
- Do not automatically publish content using the superseded Operating Partner keyword strategy.

## Required disclaimers to draft with counsel

- No guarantee of value increase, buyer interest, financing, transaction timing, or successful closing.
- Scope and limitations of any Business Value Assessment.
- Legal entity and DBA relationship.
- Jurisdiction/licensure disclosures where applicable.
- Confidentiality and data-handling limitations.
- Testimonial/case-study context and non-typical-result language where required.

## Approval record template

For each approved claim, record:

- Exact proposed wording.
- Source/evidence location.
- Evidence date and owner.
- Business owner approval date.
- Legal reviewer and date, if required.
- Expiration/reverification date.
- Pages/components where the claim may appear.

## Publication rule

When proof is not approved, remove the module or use factual process language. Do not publish placeholders, estimated network sizes, implied transaction counts, or generalized claims that a reasonable visitor would interpret as verified results.
